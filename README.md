## Literature review

Main strands covered so far:

| Strand | Focus |
|---|---|
| Mathematical modelling in teaching | Teaching functions through real-world situations |
| Learning difficulties | Misconceptions with exponential and logistic growth, covariational reasoning |
| Exponential growth bias | Why people underestimate exponential growth, and how graphs and framing affect it |
| Technology and systems dynamics | Spreadsheets, GeoGebra and Vensim for the logistic model |
